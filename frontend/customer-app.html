Next-Gen Innovation

Features That No Other Platform Has

Five breakthrough capabilities that solve what legacy platforms never could.

🚨 Critical Response

Emergency Auto-Dispatch

One-tap SOS detects your GPS location and auto-dispatches the nearest verified professional — no calls, no waiting, no friction. For genuine emergencies only: gas leaks, short circuits, burst pipes, and power failures — not everyday bookings.

  • Dispatch confirmed in under 60 seconds
  • Real-time GPS auto-match to nearest provider
  • Instant push alert sent to provider
  • Confirmed ETA before booking completes
  • Reserved for real emergencies — gas leak, short circuit, pipe burst, power failure, lockout
🚨
⚡ Response in under 60 seconds
GPS auto-matched · Provider alerted · ETA confirmed
🤖 AI-Powered

AI Cost Estimator

Our AI analyses service type, location complexity, and real market rates to deliver a transparent price range before you even confirm the booking. Zero surprises at checkout.

  • Instant price range before booking
  • Market-rate benchmarking by location
  • Complexity-based dynamic adjustment
  • No hidden charges — guaranteed
📍 Real-Time

Live Service Tracking Timeline

A live stage-by-stage timeline shows every milestone — acceptance, travel, arrival, work-in-progress — like tracking a Swiggy order, but for your home service.

  • Live map with provider route
  • Stage-by-stage push notifications
  • One-tap review on completion
  • Real-time ETA updates
📅 Smart Scheduling

Availability-Aware Booking

Providers set live availability slots. The system only shows you professionals who are actually free at your chosen time — eliminating double-bookings and cancellations entirely.

  • Real-time slot availability engine
  • Auto-conflict prevention
  • Instant reschedule suggestions
  • Provider calendar sync
📊 Admin Intelligence

Real-Time Analytics Dashboard

Admins get a live Chart.js dashboard with booking volume, revenue trends, provider performance, and platform health — all in one view, updating in real time.

  • Live booking & revenue charts
  • Provider verification pipeline
  • User activity monitoring
  • Payment analytics via Razorpay
